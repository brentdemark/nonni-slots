About this game

We built this slot machine for our grandparents. They loved playing slots on their tablet, but the popular apps kept interrupting them with pop-up ads, begged them to buy coins, and took forever to load. So we made our own: a fast, friendly slot game that's free from start to finish.

No sign-in. No personal information.

  • No account or sign-in. Open the page and start spinning.
  • No personal information collected. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Nothing to buy, ever. There's no store, and coins can't be purchased.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Coins, level and theme are saved in your own browser, not sent to us.

The ads beside and below the game help pay for the website. They never pop up over the reels or interrupt a spin. See our privacy page for details.

How to play

  1. Choose your bet with the − and + buttons.
  2. Press SPIN (or the space bar). Hold the button down to spin automatically, and press STOP to finish.
  3. Line up matching pictures on reels next to each other, starting from the left. Any row counts, so there are 243 ways to win on every spin.
  4. Wilds stand in for any picture except the bonus symbol.
  5. Three or more bonus symbols anywhere on the screen start free spins, and every win during free spins is doubled.
  6. Collect free coins from the 🎁 button every hour. Each time you go up a level you get bonus coins too, and if you ever run out, you'll get a free refill.

Tap ❔ Help at the top of the screen to see what each picture pays at your current bet.

Questions

Can I win real money?
No. Coins are pretend and are just for fun. They can't be bought, sold, cashed out or traded for prizes.
Where is my progress saved?
In the browser on the device you play on. If you switch to another device, or clear your browser's history and data, you'll start fresh with a new set of coins.
Do I need to download anything?
No. It runs in any up-to-date web browser on a tablet, phone or computer. After your first visit it opens quickly even on a slow connection.
Why are there ads?
They cover the cost of running the website, so the game can stay free without asking you to buy anything.
