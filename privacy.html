Privacy

This policy covers nonnislots.com. Last updated November 22, 2026.

What this game stores

Your coin balance, level, chosen theme and sound setting are saved in your own browser (local storage, or a cookie if local storage is unavailable). Nothing is sent to us. There are no accounts and we don't collect your name, email or payment details. Clearing your browser data resets your game.

To make the game open quickly, your browser also keeps a copy of the site's own files (an offline cache). This holds no personal information.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when you visit, to keep the service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

No real money

Coins are pretend. They can't be bought, sold, won or exchanged for money or prizes. The game is for entertainment only and is intended for adults.

Advertising

This site shows ads from Google AdSense to pay for hosting. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can also opt out of some other companies' personalised ads at aboutads.info.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you'll be asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
